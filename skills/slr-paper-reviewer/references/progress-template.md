# SLR paper-review progress

| ID | PDF | Note | Status | Decision | Relevance | Quality | Confidence | Unresolved items | Last reviewed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|

## Status values

- `Pending`: not reviewed
- `In progress`: currently being reviewed
- `Completed`: full-text review and note saved
- `Blocked—needs verification`: review could not be completed; reason required
- `Excluded`: screened out with an explicit protocol reason

## Batch completion rule

The batch is complete only when every `PXX` PDF has a row with a final status (`Completed`, `Excluded`, or `Blocked—needs verification`) and every included paper has a note under `papers/`.
