# P56 — Human and Machine: AI-Assisted Code Reviews Compared to Peers

## Screening and proposal alignment

- **Decision:** Include as supporting
- **Group:** Supporting

## Proposal RQ mapping

- **RQ1:** Excessive detail, missing context, and trust-limiting feedback are reported.
- **RQ2:** Context absence is a direct factor constraining adoption.
- **RQ3:** Covers cognitive, emotional, behavioral, trust, and adoption dimensions.
- **RQ4:** Shows cognitive-load and trust trade-offs in AI-assisted review.
- **RQ5:** Strong socio-technical and qualitative-methodology evidence.
- **RQ6:** Supports human-AI workflow and escalation design.

## Synthesis-ready summary

AI-assisted review changes engagement across cognitive, emotional, and behavioral dimensions. Engineers use similar sense-making processes for peer and LLM feedback, but adoption is constrained by trust and lack of context; excessive detail can increase cognitive load. This supports measuring reviewer burden and trust separately from correctness.
