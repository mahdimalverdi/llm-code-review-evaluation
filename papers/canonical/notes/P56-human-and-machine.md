## Synthesis-ready summary

AI-assisted review changes engagement across cognitive, emotional, and behavioral dimensions. Engineers use similar sense-making processes for peer and LLM feedback, but adoption is constrained by trust and lack of context; excessive detail can increase cognitive load. This supports measuring reviewer burden and trust separately from correctness.
