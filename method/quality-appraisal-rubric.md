# Quality-appraisal rubric (Q1–Q12)

This instrument is an extraction and interpretation aid, not an inclusion threshold. Each criterion is scored `0 = not reported`, `1 = partially reported`, or `2 = clearly reported`. A score describes the transparency and evidential usefulness of the study for this synthesis; it is not a claim that a study is generally “high quality”. Each score must be accompanied by a page, section, table, figure, or explicit `NR` note in the extraction record.

| Criterion | Question scored | Score 2 requires | Score 1 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Are the research goal and questions clear? | Goal and study questions are explicit and aligned. | Aim is recoverable but incomplete or implicit. |
| Q2 | Is the evaluated system or artifact specified? | System, task, output, and comparison are identifiable. | Some but not all are specified. |
| Q3 | Are dataset, benchmark, and input context described? | Data source, selection, and context supplied to the system are described. | One component is missing or vague. |
| Q4 | Is the generation/evaluation procedure reproducible enough to understand? | Procedure, configuration, prompts or protocol, and evaluation unit are sufficiently described. | Procedure is only partly recoverable. |
| Q5 | Are evaluation dimensions or metrics defined? | Constructs, operational definitions, and metrics are stated. | Metrics are listed but definitions or units are incomplete. |
| Q6 | Are failure or problematic-comment categories reported? | Categories or decision rules are explicit and evidenced. | Failures are discussed but not operationalized. |
| Q7 | Is the human or automated judging protocol described? | Judge identity, instructions, sample/unit, and decision procedure are reported. | The judge or procedure is only partly described. |
| Q8 | Are reliability or validity checks reported? | Agreement, calibration, validation, controls, or comparable checks are reported. | A limited check is reported without enough detail. |
| Q9 | Is a mitigation or intervention evaluated, where applicable? | Intervention and comparison/outcome are evaluated; `N/A` is recorded when not applicable. | Intervention is described but evidence is limited. |
| Q10 | Are trade-offs measured rather than merely mentioned? | At least one trade-off is operationally measured with a defined denominator/unit. | A trade-off is mentioned or only indirectly proxied. |
| Q11 | Are limitations and threats to validity discussed? | Limitations are specific to design, data, measurement, or generalization. | Limitations are generic or incomplete. |
| Q12 | Does the evidence directly support the review questions or framework? | The study provides direct, traceable evidence for at least one review output. | Relevance is adjacent or transfer requires interpretation. |

The total is the sum of Q1–Q12 (0–24). It must not be used alone to determine inclusion, and it must not be interpreted as a validated measure of study quality. RQ4 sensitivity analyses may omit Q10 and Q12 when those criteria overlap with the outcome being examined; that exclusion must be stated with the denominator.

The instrument was finalized during extraction calibration on 1 August 2026 and applied uniformly before the final synthesis. This is a protocol amendment, not evidence that the original historical search was prospectively registered. Independent reviewer agreement was not obtained; therefore the scores remain single-reviewer judgments pending calibration.
