E. LLMs in Computer Science Class
   The recent LLMs, such as the recently released ChatGPT
and GitHub Copilot, are receiving significant attention from
computer science education experts. Education experts have
found that LLMs can generate accurate solutions and accu-
rately explain code for a variety of introductory program-
ming assignments. The outstanding capabilities of these LLMs
are generating ongoing discussions about how programming
should be taught in computer science classes. At this point, the
opinions of computer science instructors are divided. Lau and
Guo [11] interviewed 20 introductory programming instructors
about how to apply it to their classes. The author responded
that in the short term, many instructors will ban the use of
LLMs in class to prevent AI-assist cheating. On the other
hand, the remaining respondents responded that they would
be willing to introduce LLMs into their classes.                                      Fig. 2. Research Methodology
