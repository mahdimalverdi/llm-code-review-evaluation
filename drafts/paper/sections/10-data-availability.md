# Data and Replication Package Availability

The bibliography lists all 121 included studies, including studies that contribute to quantitative or thematic synthesis without being cited individually in the narrative. Methodological sources cited in the report are listed in addition to the included-study inventory. The archived replication package is available through the persistent Zenodo DOI [10.5281/zenodo.18002402](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18002402). It includes stable study identifiers, bibliographic metadata, evidence tiers, extraction decisions, evidence locations, coding evidence, appraisal fields, and the dated amendment records used to reconstruct the reported counts.

The replication inventory links each included study to its evidence tier and extraction record, providing the accounting needed to interpret corpus-level counts.
