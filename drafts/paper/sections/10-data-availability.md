# Data and Replication Package Availability

The bibliography lists all 121 included studies, including studies that contribute to quantitative or thematic synthesis without being cited individually in the narrative. Methodological sources cited in the report are listed in addition to the included-study inventory. The accompanying repository contains stable study identifiers, bibliographic metadata, evidence tiers, extraction decisions, evidence locations, coding evidence, appraisal fields, the dated amendment records used to reconstruct the reported counts, and the supporting-reserve sensitivity mapping. The corpus was last frozen on 4 August 2026.

The replication-package manifest at `docs/replication-package.md` identifies the files needed for audit and reproduction. In particular, `data/search/final-corpus-freeze-register.csv` records corpus membership, `data/slr-extraction.csv` contains the generated study-level dataset, `papers/canonical/notes/` retains evidence locations and appraisal fields, and the dated amendment decisions are stored under `data/search/`. Running `python3 scripts/build_slr_dataset.py` rebuilds the extraction dataset and summary counts; `python3 scripts/build_latex.py` rebuilds the manuscript source.

A versioned public archive and an explicit artifact license have not yet been issued, so no external DOI is claimed for the present repository snapshot. The repository artifacts support inspection of the assembled corpus and the supplementary amendment but do not reconstruct the missing search history of the historical baseline.
