# Data and Replication Package Availability

The bibliography lists all 121 included studies, including studies that contribute to quantitative or thematic synthesis without being cited individually in the narrative. Methodological sources cited in the report are listed in addition to the included-study inventory. The accompanying research artifact contains stable study identifiers, bibliographic metadata, evidence tiers, extraction decisions, evidence locations, coding evidence, appraisal fields, the dated amendment records used to reconstruct the reported counts, and the supporting-reserve sensitivity mapping. The corpus was last frozen on 4 August 2026.

The replication inventory links each included study to its evidence tier and extraction record, providing the accounting needed to interpret corpus-level counts. A versioned public archive and an explicit artifact license have not yet been issued; consequently, no external DOI is claimed for this review artifact in the present version. Any submission archive should identify the exact frozen release used by the report and state its reuse license.
